## Goal
Bring `k8s-node-3` into alignment with the checked-in Talos native renderer without expanding workload isolation yet.

## Current known drift
- Remove `ExtensionServiceConfig/nut-client`.
- Move from NUT-enabled schematic image to NUT-free schematic image.

## Important notes
- Removing NUT config requires booting the NUT-free schematic image; otherwise `ext-nut-client` can block Talos boot-complete health waiting for extension service config.
- Do not enable workload isolation on `k8s-node-3` in this reconciliation pass unless a separate control-plane canary decision has been made.

## Suggested validation
- Drain one node only.
- Apply rendered config and upgrade to rendered installer image if needed.
- Verify `ext-nut-client` absent, `sandboxd` absent unless intentionally enabled, Talos health passes, pods Ready, Flux clean, Ceph `HEALTH_OK`.
